=====================================================================
 Cabinet Clear: help me test a medication comparison tool
=====================================================================
Thank you for helping! This takes about 15 minutes.

WHAT TO DO
Write 5 made-up cases. In each case, imagine a person's medicine list BEFORE a
hospital stay and the list on their NEW discharge letter. Then write what you
think changed for each medicine.

IMPORTANT
- Do NOT use any real person's information. Real medicine names are fine, real
  patients are not.
- You have not seen the app. Please don't try to make the cases easy or hard
  for it. Write them the way real lists look: brand names, abbreviations,
  different units, extra words, whatever you would really see.
- Please include a mix: some simple cases, some a careful pharmacist would
  find tricky.

WORDS TO USE AFTER "EXPECTED"
  new | stopped | dose changed | frequency changed |
  dose and frequency changed | unchanged | ask pharmacist
("ask pharmacist" = you would not be sure without checking with someone.)

WHAT TO SEND BACK
This whole file, filled in. Put your role on the AUTHOR line (for example:
pharmacist, nurse, doctor, student, parent, teacher). Your name is not needed.
